Requirement and scope revisions (against SRS v1.0, 06-08-2025)

These record decisions made by QA and relayed to development in the OBP-47 handoff on 2026-11-09. Original SRS wording is quoted so the change is traceable.

R-001 — BOOK-F-002 login identifier

  • Original SRS text: "The system shall authenticate users via email and

password, and issue a session/JWT token on success." AC-BOOK-F-002.

  • Revised requirement: The system shall authenticate users via **username

and password** and establish an authenticated Django session on success. Incorrect credentials are rejected. Email addresses are not accepted as login identifiers.

  • Reason: The implemented login form and Django authentication backend use

usernames; email remains a unique registered attribute (BOOK-F-001) and the password-reset identifier (BOOK-F-004).

  • Evidence trail: The original email-login assertion failed in run

20261103T063353040577Z (qa/history/2026-11-03_run_29049b0.md, QA-AUTH-001). It was replaced by LoginIntegrationTests.test_revised_username_and_password_establish_session and test_email_identifier_is_not_accepted_under_revised_requirement, which keep the revised behaviour explicit.

  • Status: Decided
  • Decision authority: Abdullah Faruqui (QA Lead); Deepthi A E (Test Engineer)
  • Decision date: 2026-11-09
  • Source: OBP-47 handoff message from the QA Lead to development

R-002 — BOOK-F-010 / BOOK-SR-003 payment scope

  • Original SRS text: BOOK-F-010 requires payment "through the payment

gateway"; §3.3 names a Razorpay/Stripe sandbox; §2.5 constrains payments to sandbox/test mode.

  • Revised scope: For this release, payment is the in-process

orders.services.MockPaymentGateway. It simulates initiation only; no gateway call is made, no money moves, and no card data is collected or stored.

  • QA consequence: Tests in orders.tests.CreateOrderFromCartTests are

characterization of order creation with the mock (already labelled so in qa/catalog.py). They do not verify gateway acceptance, tokenisation (BOOK-SR-003) or payment-confirmation email for BOOK-F-010. Those remain "Not verified" until a sandbox gateway is integrated.

  • Status: Decided
  • Decision authority: Abdullah Faruqui (QA Lead); Deepthi A E (Test Engineer)
  • Decision date: 2026-11-09
  • Source: OBP-47 handoff message from the QA Lead to development
